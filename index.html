PASS THROUGH – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Developer: softwartech

This policy explains how the mobile game PASS THROUGH (package com.passthrough.game) handles your information.

Information we collect

PASS THROUGH does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The game has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (scores, coins, gems, unlocked balls and upgrades, daily rewards) and settings (sound, music, vibration) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Permissions

The game only uses the vibration permission for haptic feedback. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or other personal data.

Third parties

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information under its own privacy policy when you download or update apps from Google Play. PASS THROUGH does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children

We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. If advertising, purchases or online features are added in the future, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
